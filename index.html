Privacy Policy

Hold. Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

This app does not collect anything. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no tracking, and no analytics.

Everything the app remembers is stored on your own device and nowhere else.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information locally so it does not have to ask you the same questions every time. Five things, and nothing beyond them:

  • The type of craving you chose: smoking, alcohol, food or other
  • Your timer length
  • The time of every session you finish, which is your craving log
  • Which suggestions you marked “Show more often” or “Don’t show again”
  • Which suggestions you were shown recently, so the same one does not open two sessions in a row

Your craving log is always on and there is no setting that switches it off, because the app keeps it for you rather than reporting it to anyone. It is a list of times held on your device, and it goes no further.

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else.

Deleting it

Clearing the app’s data, or uninstalling the app, removes all of it permanently. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no account to close.

Your suggestion preferences can also be undone one at a time in Settings, under “Your tips”, where everything you have marked is listed with the action that reverses it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not show advertising
  • We do not share, sell or transfer data to anyone, because we do not have any
  • We do not use cookies or any similar technology
  • We have no server for the app to send anything to

Payments

The app contains no payments of any kind. Nothing is sold, nothing is collected and there is no link out of the app to anywhere that takes money.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address and shown in the app, with a new date at the top. The date above tells you which version you are reading.
